Exactly-once as a theorem: what a CAN bus taught us about coordinating AI agents

In 1986, cars solved a multi-agent coordination problem: dozens of control units, no central computer, no unit ever addressing another. What CAN never had was memory and causality — and why adding exactly those two things turns broadcast discipline into a coordination layer for AI agents, with exactly-once claiming falling out as arithmetic instead of locks.
